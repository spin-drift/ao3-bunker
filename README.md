# AO3 Bunker

A small (but thoughtful) AO3 reading list that stays out of your way until you need it. Extremely mobile-friendly, but works on desktop too. Oh, and it also saves your scroll position.

All data is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

## Install

1. Open [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) (or your preferred userscript manager)
2. Create a new script and paste in [`ao3_bunker.user.js`](https://github.com/spin-drift/ao3-bunker/raw/refs/heads/main/ao3-bunker.user.js).
3. Go to [AO3](https://archiveofourown.org). That's it.

## Usage

Tap or click the 📦 button in the lower right of the page. Tap it again (or anywhere else) to dismiss.

On a fic, it disappears when you start scrolling, then comes back whenever you scroll up a little.

On the homepage, it'll stay visible so you can get to your fics. (You can also load any AO3 page with the reading list already popped up by adding `#bunker` to the end of the URL.)

**On mobile:** Swipe left to delete bookmarks from your list, and swipe left again to cancel (before the timer runs out). Swipe right to toggle read/unread, which works with the All/Unread filter button.

**On desktop:** Just use the buttons. :)

If you're reading a multi-chapter fic, your bookmark automatically updates to whatever chapter you're currently on (or the full work, if you switch to that).

**Keep place** is on by default. When it's enabled, your scroll position automatically updates on ANY work page, whether you've bookmarked it or not. One click turns it off.

For help (mostly just this text), click the **?** button in the upper right. :)
